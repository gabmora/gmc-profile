# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary:** Engineering hiring managers, CTOs, and founders at mid-to-enterprise companies evaluating GMC Space for custom software builds or AI product development. They arrive skeptical and want proof: architecture decisions, shipped systems, measurable results.

**Secondary:** Companies or individuals seeking licensed mobile notary services in the Hoboken/NJ area, arriving via search or referral and needing quick credibility signals (NNA certification, background check verified).

**Context:** Both audiences arrive on a laptop or phone and spend 60-90 seconds deciding whether this person is worth a conversation. They are not browsing for inspiration — they are evaluating a hire or a vendor.

## Product Purpose

GMC Space is a one-person tech agency run by Gabriela Morales. The site markets three services: custom enterprise software builds, AI SaaS products, and licensed notary services. It is not a job-seeker portfolio — it is an agency presence that demonstrates what gets built and invites qualified clients and employers to start a conversation.

Success looks like: visitors who arrive skeptical leave convinced enough to fill out the contact form or pick up the phone.

**New project to surface:** DiNi Wigs customer-facing website (Shopify-integrated, mobile-first). The footer of that client site should read: "© 2026 Dini Wigs | Powered by GMC Space" linking back to this site.

## Brand Personality

Bold, technical, polished. GMC Space sounds and looks like a studio that ships — not a freelancer fishing for gigs. Copy is specific (numbers, outcomes, stack names) and confident without being loud. The name GMC Space should appear in the site's identity, not just Gabriela Morales.

## Anti-references

- Generic bootcamp portfolios: dark blue/purple gradients, floating-bubble hero, gradient text on the name, icon grids with no differentiation.
- Feminine/pastel aesthetics: rounded-bubbly layouts, pink/lavender palettes, soft-girl visual language. The work is enterprise-grade; the site should read accordingly.
- Job-board energy: "I'm currently open to work" as the loudest signal. Confidence first, availability second.

## Design Principles

1. **Agency confidence, not candidate humility.** Frame every section as a studio that lands contracts. "I built X" becomes "GMC Space delivered X." Availability is context, not headline.
2. **Specificity is the brand voice.** 53 React components, 77 REST endpoints, 70% ops reduction — these belong in prominent positions, not buried in paragraph text. Numbers are proof; proof is the product.
3. **Two audiences, one brand.** Enterprise software clients and notary clients meet the same GMC Space identity. No visual code-switching between sections.
4. **Show, don't summarize.** Screenshots, live links, and feature demos outrank bulleted descriptions every time.
5. **Earn every word.** No restated headings, no paragraph that starts by repeating the section title. If a sentence doesn't move the reader toward a decision, cut it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Standard keyboard navigation, accessible contrast ratios. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for animations (Typed.js, counters, floating icons).
